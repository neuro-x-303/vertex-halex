Privacy Policy

Vertex HaleX ("Vertex HaleX", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data.

This Privacy Policy explains how Vertex HaleX may collect, use, store, disclose, and protect personal information when you use our websites, applications, software products, AI tools, SaaS platforms, services, or interact with us.

Because our products may have different features and data requirements, a particular product may provide additional privacy notices, consent forms, or product-specific terms. If a product-specific privacy notice conflicts with this Privacy Policy, the product-specific notice will apply to the extent of that conflict.

1. Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on the specific Vertex HaleX product or service you use.

Account and Identity Information

  • Name
  • Username
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Account credentials
  • Profile information
  • Age or date of birth where necessary
  • Organization or company information

Payment and Transaction Information

If you purchase a product or service, we may collect information relating to payment status, transaction details, payment references, order information, subscriptions, and refunds.

Payments may be processed through methods or providers such as UPI, PhonePe, YONO SBI, or other payment services depending on the product.

We generally do not directly store complete debit card or credit card numbers unless expressly stated for a particular service.

User-Generated Content

Depending on the product, you may provide text, images, videos, audio, documents, code, files, prompts, designs, messages, project data, or other content uploaded, submitted, generated, or created through our services.

Technical and Usage Information

  • IP address
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Browser type
  • Application version
  • Device identifiers
  • Approximate location derived from technical information
  • Log information
  • Usage activity
  • Crash reports
  • Performance information
  • Security and fraud-related information

2. How We Use Information

We may use personal information to:

  • Provide, operate, maintain, and improve our products and services
  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Authenticate users
  • Process payments and transactions
  • Provide customer support
  • Respond to questions and requests
  • Develop and improve software and AI systems
  • Detect and prevent fraud and abuse
  • Improve system performance
  • Communicate important service updates
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations
  • Enforce our agreements and policies
  • Protect users, systems, and Vertex HaleX

3. Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning

Some Vertex HaleX products may use artificial intelligence and machine learning technologies.

Depending on the product, AI processing may be performed using:

  • Vertex HaleX-developed AI models
  • Google Gemini or other Google AI services
  • OpenAI services
  • Other third-party AI providers
  • Cloud infrastructure and machine-learning services

Use of User Data for AI Training

Vertex HaleX does not automatically assume that customer data may be used to train AI models.

Where a product intends to use user-provided content or personal data for AI model training, improvement, evaluation, or related purposes, the relevant product documentation, privacy notice, consent mechanism, or user agreement will disclose that use.

Where applicable law requires consent or another legal basis, Vertex HaleX will obtain or rely on the appropriate legal basis before using personal data for such purposes.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We may use third-party service providers to operate our products and services.

These may include providers for:

  • Cloud hosting
  • Database infrastructure
  • Authentication
  • File storage
  • AI processing
  • Analytics
  • Security
  • Payments
  • Communications
  • Software development and deployment

Depending on the product, these providers may include Google Cloud, Google Gemini, OpenAI, Amazon Web Services (AWS), Supabase, Firebase, and other service providers.

5. Data Security

Vertex HaleX gives high priority to the security of customer and user data.

We may use appropriate security measures, including:

  • Access controls
  • Authentication systems
  • Encryption where appropriate
  • Secure cloud infrastructure
  • Security monitoring
  • Access limitation
  • Logging and monitoring
  • Backup and recovery systems

However, no system, network, application, or Internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of data, product, purpose of processing, legal requirements, and operational needs.

When information is no longer reasonably required, we may delete, anonymize, or securely dispose of it, subject to applicable legal and technical requirements.

7. Sharing and Disclosure of Information

We may disclose information:

  • To service providers that help operate our products
  • To cloud infrastructure providers
  • To AI providers where required for a product feature
  • To payment providers
  • To security and fraud-prevention providers
  • To professional advisors
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To respond to lawful government requests
  • To protect rights, safety, and property
  • In connection with a business transaction

We do not sell personal information simply because a user uses our products.

8. International Data Transfers

Because Vertex HaleX serves users worldwide and may use global cloud and technology providers, personal information may be processed or stored in countries other than the country where you live.

Where required by applicable law, we will use appropriate safeguards for international transfers of personal information.

9. Children's Privacy and Age Restrictions

Vertex HaleX products may have different age requirements.

Some products may be suitable for general audiences, while other products may be restricted to users aged 16 or 18 and above.

The applicable age requirement will be disclosed by the relevant product.

If you are a parent or legal guardian and believe that a child has provided personal information to a Vertex HaleX service in violation of applicable age restrictions, please contact us.

  • The right to access personal information
  • The right to correct inaccurate information

  • The right to restrict or object to certain processing
  • The right to withdraw consent where applicable

The availability of these rights depends on applicable law and legal exceptions.
